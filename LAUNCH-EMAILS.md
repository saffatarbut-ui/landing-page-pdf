# Waitlist launch emails

Three short emails for the people who joined the waitlist. Send them from your email tool
(the one connected to `WAITLIST.action`). Replace everything in [BRACKETS] before sending.

Timing, from launch day: **Email 1** immediately · **Email 2** two days later · **Email 3** on
the last day of the early-bird discount. Skip Email 2 and 3 for anyone who already bought
(most tools can exclude buyers if you connect Lemon Squeezy, or tag them manually).

Before sending, set up in Lemon Squeezy: a discount code (Store → Discounts) that matches the
amount and end date you promise here.
