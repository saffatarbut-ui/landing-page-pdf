The problem

The information exists. But does anyone know where it is?

Most families have the paperwork somewhere. When someone ends up in the hospital, or passes away suddenly, the one person who knew where everything was is the one nobody can ask.

  • Which accounts exist?

    Checking and savings, the 401(k) or IRA, life insurance, the subscription still charging a card every month.

  • Where are the important documents?

    The will, the power of attorney, the health care directive, the deed, the car title.

  • Who should we call?

    The doctor, the attorney, the accountant, the insurance agent, and the people who should hear it from family first.

  • What would they have wanted?

    Their care, their service, and the things they meant to say but never wrote down.

What They'll Need to Know turns that uncertainty into a plan. Fill it in once, keep it current, and tell one person where it's kept.

Questions

Before you buy

Is this a will?
No. It's an organizing tool: where things are, who to call, and what you'd want. It doesn't replace a will, a trust, or advice from an estate planning attorney. One of its main jobs is making sure your family can find the will.
What do I get, and in what format?
PDF files you can download right after checkout. What They'll Need to Know, Starting the Conversation, and What Happens Next can be filled in on screen (free Adobe Acrobat Reader works) or printed on US letter paper. The Family Document Label Pack is for printing on full-sheet sticker paper.
Will anything be mailed to me?
No. Everything is a digital download. No physical stickers will be shipped. You print the labels yourself, at home or at a print shop.
Is it safe to write all this down?
The guide is built so you never write passwords, PINs, Social Security numbers, or full account numbers in it. Instead you record where that information is stored, for example "in the password manager; the master password is with our attorney."
I'm doing this with my parents. Which should I get?
The bundle. Starting the Conversation is written for exactly this situation, with low-pressure openers and replies to the most common pushback. Many people fill out their own copy alongside their parent's, which takes the pressure off.
How long does it take?
The five "Start Here" items take about fifteen minutes. The rest can be done one section at a time, over weeks if you like. There's an update log at the end and a reminder to review it every six months.
