The problem

The information exists. But does anyone know where it is?

Most families have the paperwork somewhere. When someone ends up in the hospital or passes away suddenly, the person who knew where everything was is often the one nobody can ask.

  • Which accounts exist?

    Checking and savings, a 401(k) or IRA, life insurance, the subscription still charging a card every month.

  • Where are the important documents?

    The will, the power of attorney, the health care directive, the deed, the car title.

  • Who should we call?

    The doctor, the attorney, the accountant, the insurance agent, and the people who should hear it from family first.

  • What would they have wanted?

    Their care, their service, and the things they meant to say but never wrote down.

Doing this together

One conversation today can make things easier tomorrow.

Not every family knows where the important information is kept. This system gives you a practical way to start, organize it together, and make sure someone knows where to look.

Before you buy

Is this a legal will?

No. It's an organizing tool: where things are, who to call, and what you'd want. It doesn't replace a will, a trust, or advice from an estate planning attorney. One of its main jobs is making sure your family can find your will.

What exactly do I receive?

The Guide: What They'll Need to Know, the complete 30-page main guide, as a PDF.

The Complete Family Bundle: three PDF guides (What They'll Need to Know, Starting the Conversation, and What Happens Next) plus the Family Document Label Pack, a printable PDF with two label sheets.

Can I fill out the PDFs digitally?

Yes. The three guides have fillable fields. Open them in a PDF app that supports forms, such as the free Adobe Acrobat Reader, type your answers, and save the file.

Can I print them?

Yes. Everything is sized for US letter paper (8.5 × 11 in). Print as many copies as you need, for yourself and for each parent.

Will anything be shipped?

No. Every product is a digital download. No physical stickers or printed books will be shipped. You print the label pack yourself, on regular paper or full-sheet adhesive label paper.

How do I receive my files?

Right after payment, the checkout confirmation shows your download links, and a receipt with the same links is emailed to the address you enter at checkout. If you can't find the email, check your spam or promotions folder.

I'm preparing with my parent. Which option should I choose?

The Complete Family Bundle. Starting the Conversation is written for exactly this situation, with low-pressure openers and replies to common pushback. Many people fill out their own copy alongside their parent's, which takes the pressure off.

Is sensitive information stored by the website?

No. This website doesn't collect or store anything you write in the guides. Your completed PDFs stay wherever you save or print them.
