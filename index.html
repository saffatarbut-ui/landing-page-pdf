The problem

The information exists. But does anyone know where it is?

Most families have the paperwork somewhere. When someone is suddenly in hospital, or gone, the hard part is that the one person who knew where everything was is the one nobody can ask.

  • Which accounts exist?

    Banks, retirement accounts, life insurance, the subscription still charging a card every month.

  • Where are the important documents?

    The will, the power of attorney, the healthcare directive, the deed, the car title.

  • Who should we call?

    The doctor, the attorney, the accountant, the insurance agent, the people who should hear it from family first.

  • What would they have wanted?

    Their care, their service, and the things they meant to say but never wrote down.

What They'll Need to Know turns that uncertainty into a plan. Fill it in once, keep it current, and tell one person where it's kept.

Questions

Before you buy

Is this a will?
No. It's an organizing tool: where things are, who to call, and what you'd want. It doesn't replace a will or advice from a lawyer or financial planner. One of its main jobs is making sure your family can find the will.
What do I receive, and in what format?
PDF files, available to download right after checkout. What They'll Need to Know, Starting the Conversation, and What Happens Next can be filled in on screen or printed. The Family Document Label Pack is for printing on full-sheet sticker paper. Nothing physical is shipped.
Is it safe to write all this down?
The guide is built so you never write passwords, PINs, Social Security numbers, or full account numbers in it. Instead you record where that information is stored, for example "in the password manager; the master password is with our attorney."
I'm doing this with my parents. Which should I get?
The bundle. Starting the Conversation is written for exactly this situation, with gentle openers and replies to the most common pushback. Many people fill out their own copy alongside their parent's, which takes the pressure off.
How long does it take?
The five "Start Here" items take about fifteen minutes. The rest can be done one section at a time, over weeks if you like. There's an update log at the end and a reminder to review it every six months.
Can I print more than one copy?
Yes. Print a copy for yourself and one for each parent, and reprint whenever you update it.
